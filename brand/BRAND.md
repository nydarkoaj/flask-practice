# Ananse Technologies — Brand Guide

## Name
**Ananse** (full name **Ananse Technologies**). In Akan folklore, Kwaku Ananse the spider is the clever weaver who won the world's stories.
The story fits the business: we weave websites, ads, stock, data and deliveries into one connected business, and "the web" is literally what we build.

- Wordmark: lowercase `ananse.` with a gold full stop.
- Tagline: *We weave the web your business runs on.*
- SME line: *From first click to doorstep.*
- Sign-off: *Let's build what's next.*

Alternatives considered: **Weavr Labs**, **Kente Stack**, **Threadline Digital**.
"Ananse" is a well-known folklore name, so other businesses may already use it. Check the name and domain with the Registrar-General, GIPC/trademarks and a domain registrar before registering. A distinctive form like "Ananse Technologies" or a domain such as ananse.tech / ananseweb.com may be needed.

## Logo mark
`logo.svg`: a six-spoke spider web with a gold knot where the threads meet (the business at the centre of a connected network).
Keep clear space equal to the gold knot's diameter on every side. Minimum size 24px.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Forest | `#0E2420` | Primary background, headers, trust and growth |
| Ananse Gold | `#F2B53A` | Accent, buttons, highlights (use sparingly) |
| Jade Thread | `#2BB58A` | Data, charts, secondary labels |
| Morning Mist | `#F3F6F4` | Light backgrounds, text on forest |
| Night Canopy | `#07130F` | Dark mode background, footer |

On light backgrounds use the darker gold `#8A5C00` and jade `#137A5B` for text so it stays readable.

## Type (Google Fonts, free)

- Body: **Instrument Sans** 400/500/600
- Labels & data: **IBM Plex Mono** 500, uppercase, letter-spaced
